# quiz_game / proposal

Write `proposal.md` (`va plan` drafts it) and stop for approval. Include:

- Target length and platform profile; the style (`bold_pop` by default).
- Runtime, locked now (`ffmpeg` or `ffmpeg+hyperframes`). Never swap later without asking.
- The hook line (a question, under 8 words) and which moments flash in the teaser.
- The rounds in order with clue, answer and winner, and the expected final score.
- B-roll and sticker ideas per clue, with source (stock, library, generated) and any cost.
- The CTA (bonus clue + countdown + comment prompt).

Do not build until the user approves.
